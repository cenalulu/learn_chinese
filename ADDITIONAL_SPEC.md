# PRD: "LinguaDragon" – Immersive Chinese Learning for iPad

**Project Owner:** Junyi Lu  
**Version:** 1.0 (March 2026)  
**Target Audience:** 10-year-old students in the U.S. (Heritage & L2 Learners)  
**Core Objective:** To transform "rote memorization" of Chinese into a tactile, gamified, and culturally rich puzzle-solving experience.

---

## 1. Executive Summary
Modern Chinese education (大语文) emphasizes understanding the **logic** behind characters and the **beauty** of the language. This app bridges the gap between traditional Chinese "drill" forms and the interactive expectations of American "Gen Alpha" students by utilizing iPad-native gestures and Apple Pencil integration.

---

## 2. Target User Persona: "The Hybrid Learner"
* **Age:** 10 (5th/6th Grade).
* **Context:** Attends US public/private school; learns Chinese at home or weekend school.
* **Pain Points:** Finds traditional handwriting boring; struggles with the "logical gap" between English grammar and Chinese sentence structure; feels "idioms" are abstract and irrelevant.
* **Tech Habits:** Prefers drag-and-drop, swipe gestures, and instant feedback (Roblox/Minecraft style).

---

## 3. Functional Modules & Exercise Forms

### Module A: The Character Forge (字理实验室)
**Inspiration:** The "Zili" (字理) method—teaching characters through their evolutionary history.

| Feature | Description | iPad Interaction |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Evolution Swipe** | Users see a primitive Oracle Bone script (甲骨文) and swipe a "time slider" to see it morph into Seal script, then Regular script (楷书). | Multi-touch horizontal swipe. |
| **Radical Alchemy** | Drag different radicals (氵, 火, 亻) into a "cauldron" to see if they form a valid character. If correct, a 3D model of the object appears. | Drag-and-drop / Physics engine. |
| **Pencil Trace** | Follow a ghost-path of stroke order. Pressure sensitivity determines "ink thickness" to mimic a real brush. | Apple Pencil (Pressure/Tilt). |

### Module B: The Idiom Escape Room (成语大闯关)
**Inspiration:** "Look at pictures to guess idioms" (看图猜成语), a staple of Chinese elementary education.

* **Visual Decoding:** The app uses AI to generate stylized, modern illustrations representing an idiom (e.g., "A frog in a well"). 
* **The "Tetris" Fill-in:** Instead of typing, the four characters of the idiom fall from the top. The user must rotate and place them in the correct order in a 4-slot grid.
* **Scenario Application:** A comic strip with a blank speech bubble. The user must drag the correct idiom into the bubble to "win" the social interaction.

### Module C: Poetry Flow & Duel (古诗飞花令)
**Inspiration:** The "Feihualing" (Poetry Duel) from the *Chinese Poetry Congress*.

* **Rhythmic Tap:** Similar to *Guitar Hero*, characters of a famous poem (e.g., by Li Bai) float down. Users must tap them in the rhythm of the poem's meter.
* **Voice Duel (Siri Integration):** The AI says a keyword (e.g., "Moon" / 月). The student must recite a line containing that word. The app uses Speech-to-Text to verify accuracy.
* **Landscape Puzzle:** A beautiful "Shan-Shui" (ink wash) painting is missing pieces. Each piece is unlocked by matching a line of poetry to its visual representation (e.g., "The river flows into the sea").

### Module D: The Logic Repair Shop (病句诊疗所)
**Inspiration:** "Correcting faulty sentences" (修改病句), a core task in Chinese National Curriculum exams.

* **The "Surgery" Tool:** A sentence with Westernized grammar (e.g., "I go to school every day" translated literally as "我去学校每天") is displayed.
* **Interactions:**
    * **Cut:** Use the Pencil to "slice" a misplaced word.
    * **Suture:** Drag the word to the correct position (Subject + Time + Location + Verb).
    * **Band-age:** Replace a "weak" verb with a more descriptive one suggested by a thesaurus pop-up.

## 4. Gamification & UX Design

### 4.1. The "Ink-Wash" Progression
* **Leveling System:** Users start as a "Scholar's Assistant" and progress to "Imperial Tutor." 
* **Reward:** Completing exercises earns "Jade Coins," used to unlock virtual pets (e.g., a Pixiu or a Dragon) or custom Apple Pencil "skins" (ink brush effects).

### 4.2. Bilingual "Safety Net"
* **Instant Toggle:** Every instruction has a "Translate" button that provides English context without exiting the game.
* **Pinyin Support:** Pinyin can be toggled on/off. In "Hard Mode," Pinyin disappears to encourage character recognition.

---

## 5. Technical Requirements (iPad Specific)
* **Haptic Feedback:** Subtle vibrations when a character stroke is completed correctly.
* **Split Screen:** Support for "Research Mode" where students can have a dictionary open on the side.
* **Offline Mode:** Essential for use during car rides or flights.

---

## 6. Success Metrics
* **Engagement:** Average session length > 15 minutes.
* **Retention:** 60% of users return after the first "Poetry Duel."
* **Pedagogical Growth:** 20% increase in character recognition speed over 30 days.
